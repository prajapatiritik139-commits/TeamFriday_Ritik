Responsible use

  • Indicative only. Scores describe vocal patterns. They are not evidence of intent, honesty or guilt and must not be the basis for any decision about a person.
  • Human in the loop. The priority level helps an officer decide which call to handle first. The officer decides.
  • Privacy. Audio is analysed in memory and not stored. Logs hold only hashed call ids, scores and timings: no audio, transcripts or embeddings. Calls can be forgotten from the queue at any time.
  • Smoothing. A single window can never make a call HIGH. HIGH needs 2 of the last 3 windows to be HIGH.

Known limits

  • The model is trained on acted emotional speech (English, studio-style recordings). Real, distressed phone calls differ: accuracy on them is unknown and probably lower.
  • Five classes only: anger, fear, sad, neutral, happy. "Nervousness" has no label of its own and is approximated by the fear class.
  • The urgency signal is the probability mass on anger + fear + distress. The LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH cut-offs (40 / 70) are heuristic and not calibrated on real calls.
  • Telephone codecs, background noise and speaking style can change the output. Hindi and Gujarati emotion data was not used for training.
  • The synthetic-voice check comes from a separate service (VoiceGuard) and has its own limits: a GREEN result does not prove a caller is genuine.
