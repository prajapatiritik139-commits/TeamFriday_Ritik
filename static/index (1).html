Responsible use

Indicative onlyScores describe vocal patterns. They are not evidence of intent, honesty or guilt.
Human decidesThe level only helps an officer pick which call to handle first.
Private by designAudio is analysed in memory and never stored. Logs keep hashed ids and scores only. Any call can be forgotten.
SmoothedOne window can never make a call HIGH. It needs 2 of the last 3.
Known limits
  • Trained on acted English speech from studio-style recordings. Accuracy on real, distressed phone calls is unknown and probably lower.
  • Five classes only: anger, fear, sad, neutral, happy. Nervousness has no label of its own and is approximated by fear.
  • Urgency is the probability on anger + fear + distress. The LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH cut-offs (40 / 70) are heuristic and not calibrated on real calls.
  • Phone codecs, noise, microphone type and speaking style change the output. Hindi and Gujarati emotion data was not used.
  • The synthetic-voice module (VoiceGuard engine) has its own limits: GREEN does not prove a caller is genuine.
